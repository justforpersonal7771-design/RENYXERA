# RENYXERA — Growth, Marketing & Revenue Plan
*Zero-budget growth that attracts serious aspirants who are willing to pay. Written 26 Sep 2026.*

**North star:** paying users who stay until their exam. Every tactic here is judged on one question: does it bring serious GATE aspirants who will practise weekly and would pay ₹100–₹300 a month?

---

## 1. Who we're for (the ideal customer)

| Segment | Size | Why they pay | Where they hang out |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Final-year B.Tech, serious about GATE** | largest | Want structure, mocks and rank prediction; can't afford ₹20–40k coaching | YouTube, Telegram groups, college WhatsApp, Instagram |
| **Droppers / repeaters** | high intent | Studying full time; need analytics on their weak areas | Telegram, Reddit r/GATE, Discord |
| **Working professionals** | smaller, but have money | Short daily sessions, mobile, offline | LinkedIn, Reddit |
| **3rd-year early starters** | growing | Want a long-term plan | Instagram, YouTube Shorts |

**Positioning line:** *“Your GATE preparation OS: every PYQ solved, AI mentor, real mocks and rank prediction, for less than one coaching test series.”*

**Differentiators to repeat everywhere:**
- a real exam-interface simulator;
- the AI mentor;
- analytics of mistakes and weak topics;
- a focus planner and calendar;
- a price 10× lower than coaching.

## 3. Free acquisition channels (₹0)

### 3.1 SEO: the long-term engine (highest ROI)
- **One public page per PYQ:** `/pyq/cs/2024/q23-<slug>`. It shows the question, a solution preview, and “Practise this in the simulator”. 975 CS pages now, 8,000+ with all branches, each catching Google searches like *“GATE 2024 CS question 23 solution”*.
  - This page shows **one question** at a time and is not a downloadable bank, so the protection plan is respected.
  - Add structured data (Quiz/FAQ schema), a sitemap, internal links by topic, and a fast Cloudflare edge render.
- **Topic hubs:** “GATE CS Operating Systems PYQs – topic-wise”, with a sidebar of weightage and trend charts made from our own data. That data is unique content Google rewards.
- **Free tools** (they earn links and repeat visits):
  - GATE score calculator
  - rank predictor
  - cutoff tracker
  - marks-vs-rank tables
  - days-left countdown
  - study-plan generator
- **Articles:** “How to prepare for GATE CS in 150 days”, “Most repeated topics 2015–2026” (auto-generated from our tags).

### 3.2 YouTube + Shorts / Instagram Reels
- 3 Shorts a week: “Solve this GATE PYQ in 60 seconds”, “Trap question of the day”, “Topic weightage 2015–26 in 30s”. Record the screen of our app; that doubles as a demo.
- 1 long video a week: a subject revision, or a paper analysis during the exam.
- Every video pins a link to the matching free page (with UTM tags).

### 3.3 Telegram & WhatsApp (where GATE students actually are)
- Own a **RENYXERA GATE CS** Telegram channel: daily PYQ poll, weekly mock link, results leaderboard.
  - A bot posts automatically from our database, so it costs no time.
- Collaborate with existing large GATE groups: give admins free Pro access plus a referral code in exchange for weekly posts.

### 3.4 Reddit / Discord / Quora
- r/GATE, r/Indian_Academia, r/developersIndia (for CS/DA).
- Be genuinely helpful: answer questions and share free tools, not ads.
- A monthly “free weekend mock” post.
- A Discord server for peer study rooms (focus timers are a natural hook).

### 3.5 Campus ambassadors
- 1–2 students per college (start with ~30 target colleges: NITs, IIITs, state colleges).
- They get: free Pro, a certificate, a LinkedIn recommendation, and **20% commission** on sales through their code (paid only from revenue, so it costs nothing up front).
- They run college WhatsApp groups and put up posters for the free All-India Mock.

### 3.6 Free All-India Mock (the flagship funnel)
- A weekly Sunday mock, free for everyone, with an All-India rank and a percentile.
- Detailed analysis is free for 24 hours, then Pro.
- It generates shareable “My AIR in the RENYXERA mock” images, which is organic virality (share cards).

### 3.7 Referral loop
- Invite 3 friends who sign up → 1 month of Pro free.
- A referred user gets 10% off their first purchase.
- A leaderboard of top referrers each month.

### 3.8 Product-led growth (built into the app)
- Share cards for streaks, mock scores and topic mastery.
- A “Study with me” focus-room link.
- An end-of-exam “Your GATE year” recap, a Wrapped-style summary made to be shared.

## 4. Converting free users to paid

**Funnel targets:**

| Stage | Target |
|---|---|
| Visitor → sign-up | 8% |
| Sign-up → weekly active | 35% |
| Weekly active → paid | 5–8% |

**Free plan (generous, so people get hooked):**
- all PYQs with answers;
- 3 AI mentor messages a day;
- 1 free mock a week;
- basic analytics.

**Pro plan (clear upgrades):**
- the practice bank;
- all mocks and subject tests;
- unlimited AI mentor (fair-use cap);
- full analytics, weak-topic plans and the rank predictor;
- offline Downloads;
- no limits.

**Conversion moments** (show an upgrade prompt only at these points):
- after the first mock result (“See your full analysis”);
- when the AI daily quota is hit;
- when opening the practice bank;
- in January (“Final 30 days pack”).

**Email/notifications:**
- A welcome series of 5 emails over 10 days.
- A weekly progress email.
- Win-back after 7 inactive days.
- Resend free tier (3,000 emails a month).

| Plan | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|

- Student verification (college email) → 20% off.

**Payments:**
- **Razorpay**: no setup fee; ~2% per transaction; UPI; an individual can start with PAN + bank KYC (upgrade to a sole proprietorship/GST once turnover requires it).
- Keep Stripe as the option for international users later.
- Only per-transaction fees, so this fits the ₹0 budget.

## 9. Metrics to watch weekly (a free dashboard)

**Tools** (all free tiers):
- Cloudflare Web Analytics;
- PostHog free tier for product analytics;
- Google Search Console for SEO;
- a Supabase SQL view for revenue.

**Metrics:**
- **Acquisition:** visitors by channel, and signups by channel (UTM).
- **Activation:** percentage who finish their first test within 24 hours.
- **Retention:** D7 and D30 retention; weekly active users.
- **Revenue:** paid conversion percentage, MRR, refund percentage, and revenue by plan.
- **Content:** PYQ page impressions and clicks (Search Console).

**Rule:** double down on any channel with a signup cost below ₹20-equivalent in effort-hours, and drop channels that give nothing after 4 weeks.

## 10. Things we will NOT do

- Buy fake followers or reviews, or spam groups.
- Share our question bank as PDFs to “go viral”, which would break the protection strategy. We share **single questions and tools** only.
- Pay for ads before organic conversion is proven.
