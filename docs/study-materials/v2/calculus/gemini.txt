### Chapter 01 · Limits, Continuity and Differentiability *(57% of Calculus Marks!)*

#### Lesson 01.1 · Limits (`LHL = RHL`), 7 Indeterminate Forms, L'Hôpital & Standard Shortcuts
- **Tag:** `[MUST]` — Highest-yield Calculus skill (`2026_FN_Q32`, `2022_FN_Q34`, `2021_FN_Q54`, `2019_FN_Q34`, `2017_FN_Q42`).
- **Estimated reading time:** 75 minutes.
- **Prerequisites:** Lesson 00.1.
- **Beginner must-see:** $\lim_{x \to a} f(x)$ does NOT ask what happens *standing AT `x = a`* — it asks *"as two hikers walk toward `a` from the Left (`LHL`) and Right (`RHL`), what same height `L` are both walking toward?"* When plugging `x = a` produces a disguise like `0/0` (or `\infty/\infty`, `1^\infty`), you unwrap the disguise using **Factoring, Rationalizing, Standard Limits, or L'Hôpital (`f'/g'`)**!
- **Finer points:**
  - **Existence (`LHL = RHL = finite`):** Notice `e^{1/x}` (`0` vs `+\infty`) and `\ln(1/x)` (`-> +\infty` as `x -> 0^+`, `2026_FN_Q32`).
  - **The 7 Indeterminate Forms:** `0/0, \infty/\infty, 0\cdot\infty, \infty-\infty, 1^\infty, 0^0, \infty^0` (`0/0` can equal anything — `0`, `108/7`, `DNE`!).
  - **4 Solving Tools:**
    1. **Factoring (`2019_FN_Q34`)** & **Rationalizing (`2021_FN_Q54`: multiply by `\sqrt{2x+22} + 4`)**.
    2. **Standard 1-Line Limits & Substitution (`2022_FN_Q34`):** `u = \sqrt{x} -> 0^+` turns `\sqrt{x}/(1 - e^{2\sqrt{x}})` into `-1/2`; `(1 + a/x)^x -> e^a`; **`1^\infty` shortcut = $e^{\lim g(x)(f(x)-1)}$**!
    3. **L'Hôpital's Rule (`2021_FN_Q54`, `2019_FN_Q34`, `2017_FN_Q42`):** ONLY on `0/0` or `\infty/\infty`; differentiate top and bottom **separately (`f'/g'`)**, NEVER Quotient Rule; watch infinite oscillation failure (`(x + \sin x)/x` as `x -> \infty`).
    4. **Sign Traps (`x -> -\infty` with `\sqrt{x^2} = |x| = -x`) & Squeeze Theorem (`x^2 \sin(1/x) -> 0`).**
- **GATE-level items:** `\sqrt{x}` exponential limit (`2022_FN_Q34`: `-0.5`), radical limit (`2021_FN_Q54`: `0.25`), rational `0/0` (`2019_FN_Q34`: `108/7`), degree-7 L'Hôpital (`2017_FN_Q42`: `1`), and `\ln(1/x)` forcing `c_2 = 0` (`2026_FN_Q32`).

### Chapter 02 · Maxima, Minima and Monotonicity

#### Lesson 02.1 · Monotonicity (`f' >= 0`), Local vs Global Extrema & Derivative Tests
- **Tag:** `[MUST]` — Covers `2026_FN_Q46(A, B)`, `2023_FN_Q28`, `2020_FN_Q17`.
- **Estimated reading time:** 65 minutes.
- **Prerequisites:** Lesson 00.1 (Wavy Curve), Lesson 01.2.
- **Beginner must-see:** Where is a roller coaster climbing (**Increasing**: `slope f'(x) >= 0`), falling (**Decreasing**: `f'(x) <= 0`), or hitting a hilltop (**Local Max**: `+` slope switching to `-` slope) or valley (**Local Min**: `-` switching to `+`)? Be careful: a function can have BOTH a local hilltop AND a local valley (`2023_FN_Q28`) even if its ends shoot off to `±\infty`, and **`f(1) > f(0)` does NOT guarantee it climbs the entire way** (`2020_FN_Q17`: `x^2 - sin x` dips down first near `0`!)!
- **Finer points:**
  - **Monotonicity (`2020_FN_Q17`):** Strictly increasing on `[a, b]` $\iff f'(x) \ge 0$ everywhere on `(a, b)` and not flat on any subinterval (isolated `f'(0) = 0` OK for `\sqrt{x^3 + 1}`!).
  - **Critical Points:** Where **`f'(x) = 0`** OR **`f'(x)` does NOT exist** (e.g. `|x|` at `0`).
  - **Tests for Local Extrema:**
    - **1st Derivative Sign-Change Test (`2026_FN_Q46`):** `+ -> -` (Local Max), `- -> +` (Local Min), same sign (`x^3` Saddle/Inflection!). Works even when `f''(c)` fails to exist!
    - **2nd Derivative Test (`2023_FN_Q28`):** At `f'(c) = 0`, `f''(c) < 0` -> Local Max, `f''(c) > 0` -> Local Min, `f''(c) = 0` -> inconclusive (check higher derivatives or 1st deriv!).
  - **Local vs Global Extrema (`[a, b]` endpoints vs $\mathbb{R}$ unbounded, `2023_FN_Q28`).**
  - **Word Optimization & Multivariable Hessian `rt - s^2` (`[LATER]` light note).**
- **GATE-level items:** Increasing everywhere on `[0, 1]` (`2020_FN_Q17`: `III` only), cubic local max/min existence (`2023_FN_Q28`: B, D), and non-`C^2` global/local max at `0` (`2026_FN_Q46`: A).
- **Diagrams needed:** `GRAPHPIC` (Cubic `2023_FN_Q28` hill/valley vs `x^3` saddle vs `x^2 - \sin x` initial dip `2020_FN_Q17` + cup-up `f''>0` / cup-down `f''<0`).
- **Hand traces:** Tracing `f'(x)` signs across `[-15, 5]` for `2023_FN_Q28`, testing `2x - \cos x` at `x = 0` (`2020_FN_Q17`), and verifying `f(x) <= 0 = f(0)` (`2026_FN_Q46`).
- **Common mistakes:** Checking only `f(1) > f(0)` for "increasing everywhere on `[0, 1]`" (`2020_FN_Q17(II)`!); thinking `"no global max"` means `"no local max"` (`2023_FN_Q28(A)`!); getting stuck when `f''(0)` doesn't exist (`use 1st derivative sign or direct inequality f(x) <= 0`, `2026_FN_Q46`).

- **Estimated reading time:** 75 minutes.
- **Prerequisites:** Lesson 01.2 (Derivatives), Lesson 03.1.
- **Beginner must-see:** Integration is the exact **Reverse Gear** of differentiation — finding total area (`distance = \int speed dt`) instead of slope! Three clean tools handle 95% of GATE integrals: **Substitution** (reverse Chain Rule: `u = x^2`, `2018_FN_Q26`), **Integration by Parts (`ILATE`: reverse Product Rule**, `2025_AN_Q12`), and **Partial Fractions**!

#### Lesson 04.2 · Definite Integral Properties (`Even/Odd`, `King's Rule`) & Multiple (`Double/Triple`) Integrals
- **Tag:** `[MUST]` — High-speed 1-mark & 2-mark tricks (`2026_AN_Q27`, `2024_AN_Q35`, `2023_FN_Q31`).
- **Estimated reading time:** 70 minutes.
- **Prerequisites:** Lesson 00.1 (Even/Odd), Lesson 04.1.
- **Beginner must-see:** Never brute-force a definite integral before checking its limits! If limits say **`[-a, +a]`**, EVERY **odd** term instantly vanishes to **`0`** (`2026_AN_Q27`, `2023_FN_Q31`)! And if an unknown function `f(x)` satisfies `f(x) + f(a+b-x) = const`, **King's Property** mirrors `[a, b]` onto itself and solves the integral in 2 lines WITHOUT knowing `f(x)` (`2024_AN_Q35`)!
- **Finer points:**
  - **6 Definite Integral Laws:**
    1. Interval split at corners (`|x|` or piecewise joins).
    2. **Symmetric `[-a, a]` (`2026_AN_Q27`, `2023_FN_Q31`):** `Odd -> 0`, `Even -> 2 \int_0^a` (verify limits ARE symmetric `[-a, a]`!).
    3. **King's Property (`2024_AN_Q35`):** $\int_a^b f(x)\,dx = \int_a^b f(a + b - x)\,dx$ (`I = 1`).
    4. Queen's Property (`[0, 2a]`).
