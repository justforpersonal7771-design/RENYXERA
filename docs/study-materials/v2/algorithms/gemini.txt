### Chapter 00 · Math Toolbox for Algorithms *(New Prerequisite Chapter)*

#### Lesson 00.1 · Logarithms, Exponents & Essential Algorithm Series
- **Tag:** `[MUST]` — Zero-level readers cannot parse `2^{\log n}`, `n^{\log_4 5}`, `\log(n!)`, or loop sums without this warm-up (`2026_FN_Q17`, `2026_AN_Q24`, `2021_FN_Q61`, `2017_AN_Q61`).
- **Estimated reading time:** 55 minutes.
- **Prerequisites:** High-school powers (`2^3 = 8`).
- **Beginner must-see:** Think of $\log_2 n$ simply as **"How many times can I cut $n$ in half until I hit 1?"** For $n = 1024$, cutting in half 10 times hits `1`, so $\log_2 1024 = 10$! That explains why $2^{\log_2 n}$ literally undoes the cut and gives $n$ back (`2026_AN_Q24`)!
- **Finer points:**
  - **6 Log/Exponent Rules:** $b^{\log_b n} = n$, **Swap identity $a^{\log_b c} = c^{\log_b a}$**, $\log_a n = \frac{\log_2 n}{\log_2 a}$, $\log(xy) = \log x + \log y$, $\log(n^k) = k \log n$, **Stirling's Approximation $\log_2(n!) = \Theta(n \log n)$** (`2026_AN_Q24`).
  - **Comparing exponents:** $n^{\log_4 5}$ vs $n^2$ (`4^2 = 16 > 5`, so $\log_4 5 < 2$, `2026_FN_Q17`!); taking $\log$ of both sides to compare `n^{\log n}` vs `n^{\sqrt{n}}` vs `10^n` (`2021_FN_Q61`).
  - **5 Essential Summations:**
    1. Arithmetic: $\sum_{i=1}^n i = \frac{n(n+1)}{2} = \Theta(n^2)$.
    2. Shrinking Geometric (`r < 1`): $n + n/2 + n/4 + \dots = \Theta(n)$ (`2023_FN_Q54`).
    3. Growing Geometric (`r > 1`): $1 + 2 + 4 + \dots + 2^k = 2^{k+1}-1 = \Theta(2^k)$.
    4. Arithmetico-Geometric: $\sum_{k=1}^n k \cdot 2^n$ vs $\sum_{k=1}^n k / 2^k$ (`2025_FN_Q20`).
    5. Harmonic: $H_n = \sum_{i=1}^n \frac{1}{i} = \ln n + O(1) = \Theta(\log n)$ (`2017_AN_Q61`).
- **GATE-level items:** Ranking `n^{1/3}, log n, log(n!), 2^{log n}` (`2026_AN_Q24`), `n^{\log n}, n^{\sqrt{n}}, 10^n` (`2021_FN_Q61`), and `100/n, 10, log n, sqrt(n), n` (`2017_FN_Q21`).
- **Diagrams needed:** `ARRAY` + `COMPARE` (Staircase AP box, Shrinking GP unit square, and Harmonic area bars); `TRACE` (Taking logs step-by-step to rank `f_1, f_2, f_3` in `2021_FN_Q61`).
- **Hand traces:** Evaluating `2^{\log_2 32}`, comparing `n^{\log_4 5}` vs `n^2`, summing `n + n/2 + ...` for `n = 16`, and simplifying $\log(n!)$ via pairing $1 \times n$.
- **Common mistakes:** Thinking `2^{\log n}` is exponential (`2^{\log_2 n} = n` linear! `2026_AN_Q24`); thinking `n + n/2 + n/4 + ...` is $n \log n$ instead of $2n = \Theta(n)$ (`2023_FN_Q54`); thinking `log(n!)` is $\Theta(\log n)$ (`2026_AN_Q24`).

### Chapter 01 · Asymptotics, Loop Complexity and Recurrences

#### Lesson 01.1 · Asymptotic Notations (`O, \Omega, \Theta, o, \omega`) & Space vs Time Complexity
- **Tag:** `[MUST]` — Foundation of `19/73` Algorithms PYQs (`2024_FN_Q20`, `2023_FN_Q29`, `2022_FN_Q11`, `2021_FN_Q61`, `2017_FN_Q21`).
- **Estimated reading time:** 65 minutes.
- **Prerequisites:** Lesson 00.1.
- **Beginner must-see:** Big-O is like saying *"at most (`<=`)"* for very large inputs, $\Omega$ is *"at least (`>=`)"*, and $\Theta$ is *"exact tight fit (`==`)"* — ignoring constant multipliers like `100n` vs `2n` (`2023_FN_Q54`)! Watch out: **Worst-Case** is WHICH input you test (the nastiest one!), while **$O / \Omega / \Theta$** are just math symbols describing that curve (`2024_FN_Q20`)!
- **Finer points:**
  - Formal definitions (`\exists c, n_0` vs `\forall c \exists n_0` for `o, \omega`).
  - **The Limit Ratio Test:** $\lim_{n\to\infty} \frac{f(n)}{g(n)}$ (`0 => o(g) and O(g)`, `finite > 0 => \Theta(g)`, `\infty => \omega(g) and \Omega(g)`).
  - **5 Classic Traps:**
    - If $f = \Theta(g)$, then $f = O(g)$ AND $f = \Omega(g)$ (`2024_FN_Q20`).
    - $n \in o(n^2)$ AND $n \in O(n^2)$, NOT $\Theta(n^2)$ (`2023_FN_Q29`).
    - Polynomial vs Exponential under $f(n^2)$ vs $f(n)^2$ (`2022_FN_Q11`: holds for polynomials, fails for $2^n$!).
    - Decreasing function `100/n` is $o(1)$ — strictly smaller than constant `10` (`2017_FN_Q21`!).
    - Constants $c$ CANNOT depend on $n$, and $f(n) = 2^{n+1}$ IS $\Theta(2^n)$, whereas $2^{2n} = 4^n \neq O(2^n)$!
  - **Time vs Auxiliary Space Complexity:** Recursion call-stack depth counts toward space complexity!
- **GATE-level items:** Ranking growth functions (`2026_AN_Q24`, `2021_FN_Q61`, `2017_FN_Q21`), `O/o/\Omega/\Theta` MSQs (`2023_FN_Q29`), worst-case `O(N) & \Omega(N)` (`2024_FN_Q20`), and `f(n^2)` transformations (`2022_FN_Q11`).
- **Diagrams needed:** `COMPARE` (`2 × 3` Scenarios vs Bounds grid + 5 speed meters `o, O, \Theta, \Omega, \omega`); `FLOW` (Limit-Ratio test decision tree).
- **Hand traces:** Testing $f(n)=n, g(n)=n^2$ (`2023_FN_Q29`), $f(n^2)$ for $n^3$ vs $2^n$ (`2022_FN_Q11`), and limit-comparing $n^{\log n}$ vs $n^{\sqrt{n}}$ (`2021_FN_Q61`).
- **Common mistakes:** Confusing $\Omega$ with "Best Case" (`2024_FN_Q20`!); thinking $f \in o(g)$ prevents $f \in O(g)$ (`2023_FN_Q29`!); claiming $2^{2n} = \Theta(2^n)$; forgetting recursion stack in space complexity.

#### Lesson 01.2 · Loop Complexity Analysis (Translating Code into Sums)
- **Tag:** `[MUST]` — High-ROI guaranteed questions (`2023_FN_Q54`, `2018_FN_Q42`, `2018_FN_Q55`, `2017_AN_Q61`).
- **Estimated reading time:** 60 minutes.
- **Prerequisites:** Lesson 00.1, Lesson 01.1, C5 (loops).
- **Beginner must-see:** To find how fast a loop runs, don't guess "2 nested loops = $n^2$!" Instead, ask ONE simple question: **for each step of the outer loop (`i = 1, 2, 3...`), how many steps does the inner loop actually take?** Then add those counts up!
- **Finer points:**
  - Single loops: `i++` ($n$), `i += k` ($n/k$), `i *= 2` ($\log n$), `i = i * i` ($\log\log n$), `i*i <= n` ($\sqrt{n}$).
  - Dependent nested loops: `j <= i` ($\Theta(n^2)$), `n` then `n/2` then `n/4` ($\Theta(n)$, `2023_FN_Q54`!), **`j += i` ($\sum n/i = \Theta(n \log n)$, `2017_AN_Q61`!)**, sequential halving loops ($\log n$ then $\log\log n$, `2018_FN_Q42`!).
  - Multi-parameter recursive loops: `Count(x, y)` (`2018_FN_Q55`: `y` drops by 1 -> `1023` outer rounds × `10` halvings of `x`!).
  - Early exit worst-case (`2024_FN_Q20`): check input that delays `break`/`return` until the very last element!
- **GATE-level items:** Computing exact execution counts (`2018_FN_Q42`, `2018_FN_Q55`) and tight $\Theta$ bounds on nested halving/harmonic loops (`2023_FN_Q54`, `2017_AN_Q61`).
- **Diagrams needed:** `TRACE` (6-pattern Loop Trace Table: `i` value, inner count, total sum); `CALLTREE` (`Count(1024, 1024)` showing `1023` resets of `x=1024 -> 1`, `2018_FN_Q55`).
- **Hand traces:** Tracing `2023_FN_Q54`, `2017_AN_Q61` (`n=8`: `8 + 4 + 3 + 2 + 2 + 2 + 2 + 2`), `2018_FN_Q42` (`5`), and `2018_FN_Q55` (`10230`).
- **Common mistakes:** Blindly writing $\Theta(n^2)$ for two nested `for` loops (`2017_AN_Q61` is $n \log n$!) or $\Theta(n \log n)$ for `while(n > 1) { for(1..n); n /= 2; }` (`2023_FN_Q54` is $\Theta(n)$!); miscounting `y != 1` off by 1 (`1023` vs `1024` in `2018_FN_Q55`!).

#### Lesson 01.3 · Solving Recurrences: Master Theorem, Recursion Trees, Unrolling & Variable Substitution
- **Tag:** `[MUST]` — Tested across nearly every GATE paper (`2026_FN_Q17`, `2026_AN_Q25`, `2025_FN_Q20`, `2024_FN_Q57`, `2021_FN_Q50`, `2021_AN_Q55`, `2020_FN_Q34`, `2017_AN_Q50`).
- **Estimated reading time:** 90 minutes.
- **Prerequisites:** Lesson 00.1, Lesson 01.1, DS D5 (Recursion Trees).
- **Beginner must-see:** Master Theorem is just a tug-of-war between **Leaves** (total subcalls $n^{\log_b a}$) and **Root work** ($f(n)$)! Whoever is polynomially bigger wins; if they tie, multiply by $\log n$! And when a weird recurrence breaks Master Theorem (`T(n-1)`, `T(\sqrt{n})`, or `T(n/2) + T(2n/5)`), just draw the **Recursion Tree** or swap **`n = 2^m`**!
- **Finer points:**
  - **Master Theorem ($T(n) = a T(n/b) + f(n)$, $a \ge 1, b > 1$):**
    - Compare $n^{\log_b a}$ vs $f(n)$: Case 1 (polynomially smaller -> $n^{\log_b a}$, `2021_AN_Q55(C)`), Case 2 (including $\log^k n$ -> $n^{\log_b a} \log^{k+1} n$), Case 3 (polynomially larger + regularity condition -> $f(n)$).
    - **Master Theorem Traps:** $n / \log n$ sits in the non-polynomial gap between Case 1 & 2 (`2021_AN_Q55(B)` -> $\Theta(n \log \log n)$ for $2T(n/2) + n/\log n$!); floors/ceilings inside `T(\lceil n/b \rceil)` don't change $\Theta$; **Coupled recurrences `T_1, T_2`** solved bottom-up (`2026_FN_Q17`).
  - **Linear Recurrence Unrolling (`T(n - c)`):** `T(n-1) + 1 -> \Theta(n)`, `T(n-1) + n -> \Theta(n^2)` (`2026_AN_Q25`), **`T(n) = 2T(n-1) + n 2^n -> \Theta(n^2 2^n)` (`2025_FN_Q20`)**!
  - **Unequal-Split Recursion Tree & Akra-Bazzi Intuition (`2021_FN_Q50`):** $T(\lceil n/2 \rceil) + T(\lfloor 2n/5 \rfloor) + 7n$: level sum decays by $9/10 < 1 \implies \Theta(n)$! (Contrast with `n/3 + 2n/3 = 1 \implies \Theta(n \log n)`!).
  - **Change-of-Variable Substitution (`\sqrt{n}`, `n^{1/a}`):**
    - $T(n) = 2 T(\sqrt{n}) + 1$ (`n = 2^m -> S(m) = 2 S(m/2) + 1 -> \Theta(\log n)`, `2017_AN_Q50`).
    - Divide first! $T(n) = \sqrt{n} T(\sqrt{n}) + n \implies \frac{T(n)}{n} = \frac{T(\sqrt{n})}{\sqrt{n}} + 1 \implies \Theta(n \log \log n)$ (`2024_FN_Q57`!).
    - $T(n) = T(n^{1/a}) + 1 \implies \Theta(\log_a \log_b n)$ (`2020_FN_Q34`!).
  - **Amortized Analysis Intuition (`[SHOULD]`):** Aggregate & Potential method on Binary Counter increment ($O(1)$ amortized).
- **GATE-level items:** Coupled Master Theorem (`2026_FN_Q17`), non-polynomial gap (`2021_AN_Q55`), unequal splits (`2021_FN_Q50`), $\sqrt{n}$ substitution (`2024_FN_Q57`, `2017_AN_Q50`), $n^{1/a}$ (`2020_FN_Q34`), and exponential unrolling (`2025_FN_Q20`).
- **Diagrams needed:** `TREE` (3 Master Theorem tug-of-war trees + unequal `9/10` decaying tree for `2021_FN_Q50`); `FLOW` (Master Theorem + Change-of-Variable selector flowchart); `TRACE` (Unrolling `2T(n-1) + n 2^n` level by level, `2025_FN_Q20`).
- **Hand traces:** Solving ALL 6 styles (`2026_FN_Q17`, `2025_FN_Q20`, `2024_FN_Q57`, `2021_FN_Q50`, `2020_FN_Q34`, `2017_AN_Q50`) line by line.
- **Common mistakes:** Applying Master Case 2 when $f(n) = n / \log n$ (`2021_AN_Q55(B)`!); forgetting `n` division before substituting $n = 2^m$ in `2024_FN_Q57`; missing the extra `n` factor in $T(n) = 2T(n-1) + n 2^n$ (`2025_FN_Q20` gives $n 2^n$ vs $n^2 2^n$!); forgetting `\log n` in Case 2 (`2021_AN_Q55(D)`!).
