#### Lesson 01.2 · Signed Integers (`Sign-Mag`, `1's`, `2's Complement`) & Sign Extension
- **Tag:** `[MUST]` — Prerequisite for Adders, Subtractors & Signed Overflow (`2025_FN_Q25`, `2019_FN_Q17`).
- **Estimated reading time:** 55 minutes.
- **Prerequisites:** Lesson 01.1.
- **Beginner must-see:** Computers only store `0`s and `1`s — there is NO `-` key in hardware! Early designs used the leftmost bit as a `-` sticker (**Sign-Magnitude**), which accidentally created TWO zeros (`+0` and `-0`) and forced separate subtraction circuits! **2's Complement** fixes both by giving the leftmost bit a **negative weight (`-2^{n-1}`)** — leaving ONE zero (`0000`) and letting plain addition handle subtraction too!

#### Lesson 02.2 · Canonical Forms (`SOP`/`POS`), Dual vs Complement & Counting Boolean Functions

#### Lesson 05.1 · Latches vs Flip-Flops (`SR, D, JK, T`), Excitation Tables, Conversions & Timing

### Chapter 06 · Computer Arithmetic: Fixed and Floating Point

#### Lesson 06.1 · Fixed-Point Fractions, Signed / Sign-Magnitude Overflow & Saturation
- **Tag:** `[MUST]` — Very high frequency (`2026_FN_Q22`, `2026_AN_Q28`, `2024_FN_Q18`, `2022_FN_Q18`, `2019_FN_Q33`, `2018_FN_Q43`, `2017_FN_Q11`, `2017_FN_Q24`).
- **Estimated reading time:** 65 minutes.
- **Prerequisites:** Lesson 01.2, Lesson 04.1 (Adders).
- **Beginner must-see:** Fixed-size registers (`4`, `8`, `32` bits) have strict speed-limit walls (`-8..+7` for 4-bit 2's complement)! Whenever an addition or subtraction pushes the true mathematical answer outside those walls, the sign bit flips into lies (**Arithmetic Overflow**, `2026_AN_Q28`, `2017_FN_Q11`) — and in **Fixed-Point Fractions**, decimal numbers like `0.1` NEVER fit cleanly unless their fraction is built strictly out of `1/2, 1/4, 1/8, ...` (`2018_FN_Q43`)!
- **Finer points:**
  - **Unsigned Fixed-Point `i . f` (`2017_FN_Q24`, `2018_FN_Q43`):** Range `0` to $2^i - 2^{-f}$; exact decimal representability iff reduced fraction has power-of-2 denominator $\le 2^f$ (`12.1` & `3.001` fail `2018_FN_Q43`!).
  - **2's Complement Overflow Rules (`2026_AN_Q28`, `2024_FN_Q18`, `2022_FN_Q18`, `2017_FN_Q11`):**
    1. Range check: sum/diff lies outside `[-2^{n-1}, +2^{n-1} - 1]`.
    2. Sign-flip check: `(+)+(+) -> (-)` or `(-)+(-) -> (+)` (`A_{n-1} B_{n-1} \bar{S}_{n-1} + \bar{A}_{n-1} \bar{B}_{n-1} S_{n-1}`, `2017_FN_Q11`).
    3. Carry check: **`C_{n-1} ⊕ C_n`** (NEVER `C_n` alone! `2017_FN_Q11(A)` trap!).
    - Remember: `A - B` in 2's complement adds `A + (-B)`, so watch for `-(-2^{n-1})` (`-(-8)` overflows 4 bits)!
  - **Sign-Magnitude Arithmetic & Overflow (`2026_FN_Q22`, `2019_FN_Q33`):** `n`-bit magnitude is `0..2^{n-1}-1`; adding two same-sign numbers (or `X - Y` with opposite signs!) can hit $2(2^{n-1} - 1)$, requiring **`n + 1` bits** to never overflow (`2019_FN_Q33`)!
- **GATE-level items:** Sign-Mag overflow MSQ (`2026_FN_Q22`) & bit width (`2019_FN_Q33`: `n + 1`), 4-bit/5-bit 2's complement overflow (`2026_AN_Q28`, `2024_FN_Q18`, `2022_FN_Q18`), hardware overflow gate (`2017_FN_Q11`), fixed-point range (`2017_FN_Q24`), and exact decimal check (`2018_FN_Q43`).
- **Diagrams needed:** `MEMORY` (Number line showing 4-bit `[-8, +7]` overflow cliffs + unsigned `i.f` bit weights); `CIRCUIT` (8-bit RCA with `C_6 ⊕ C_7` and `A_7 B_7 S_7'` overflow gates, `2017_FN_Q11`).
- **Hand traces:** Tracing `2026_FN_Q22` (`X = -52, Y = +76 -> X - Y = -128` overflows 8-bit Sign-Mag `[-127, +127]`!), `2026_AN_Q28` (`B, D`), `2024_FN_Q18` (`A - B = +16`), and `2018_FN_Q43`.
- **Common mistakes:** Checking `C_out = 1` alone for 2's complement overflow (`2017_FN_Q11(A)`!); confusing `8-bit Sign-Mag` min (`-127`) with `8-bit 2's Comp` min (`-128`) in `2026_FN_Q22` (`-128` DOES overflow Sign-Magnitude!); forgetting subtraction `X - Y` overflows when signs are OPPOSITE (`2026_FN_Q22`, `2024_FN_Q18`).

#### Lesson 06.2 · IEEE 754 Floating-Point Format (`Single/Double`) & Arithmetic (`+, ×, ÷`)
- **Tag:** `[MUST]` — Highest-density Hard/Medium NAT & MSQ unit (`2026_AN_Q34`, `2026_FN_Q36`, `2025_AN_Q49`, `2024_AN_Q23`, `2023_FN_Q45`, `2022_FN_Q41`, `2021_AN_Q17`, `2021_FN_Q65`, `2020_FN_Q59`, `2017_AN_Q12`).
- **Estimated reading time:** 90 minutes.
- **Prerequisites:** Lesson 06.1.
- **Beginner must-see:** Fixed-point cannot hold both atom sizes ($10^{-15}$) and galaxy distances ($10^{25}$)! **IEEE 754 Floating Point** stores scientific notation in binary: **`(-1)^S × (1.M) × 2^{E - 127}`**! Why store `E + 127` (`Biased`) instead of 2's complement? Because biased `E` lets hardware sort positive floats using a plain unsigned integer comparator — and `E = 0` is specially reserved for `0.0` and tiny subnormals, making **`E = 1, M = 0`** the smallest normalized positive number (`2^{-126}`, `2021_AN_Q17`)!
- **Finer points:**
  - **Single Precision (`32-bit: 1 S | 8 E [bias 127] | 23 M`) & Double Precision (`64-bit: 1 S | 11 E [bias 1023] | 52 M`).**
  - **The 5 IEEE 754 Regions:**
    1. `E = 0, M = 0` -> `±0.0`.
    2. `E = 0, M != 0` -> **Denormalized/Subnormal**: `(-1)^S × (0.M) × 2^{-126}` (`2^{-149}` smallest subnormal).
    3. `1 <= E <= 254` -> **Normalized**: `(-1)^S × (1.M) × 2^{E - 127}` (smallest positive normalized = **`E = 00000001, M = 0`**, `2021_AN_Q17`!).
    4. `E = 255, M = 0` -> `±∞`.
    5. `E = 255, M != 0` -> `NaN`.
  - **Top-3-Hex-Digit Speed-Read (`2026_AN_Q34`, `2025_AN_Q49`, `2021_FN_Q65`, `2017_AN_Q12`):** Decode bits `[31..20]` (`S | 8-bit E | top 3 bits of M`) in 10 seconds!
  - **IEEE 754 Arithmetic (`2026_FN_Q36`, `2023_FN_Q45`, `2020_FN_Q59`):**
    - **Add/Sub (`2026_FN_Q36`):** Align smaller exponent to larger (`shift 1.M right by ΔE`), add/sub, normalize back to `1.xxxx`.
    - **Multiply (`2023_FN_Q45`):** `S = S_1 ⊕ S_2`, `E = E_1 + E_2 - 127`, multiply `(1.M_1) × (1.M_2)` & normalize (`if >= 2, >>1 & E++`).
    - **Divide (`2020_FN_Q59`):** `S = S_1 ⊕ S_2`, `E = E_1 - E_2 + 127`, divide `(1.M_1) / (1.M_2)` & normalize!
- **GATE-level items:** Hex <-> Decimal (`2026_AN_Q34`: `-60.25`, `2025_AN_Q49`, `2021_FN_Q65`: `-7.75`, `2017_AN_Q12`: `2.27×10^{-1}`), smallest normalized (`2021_AN_Q17` — fix key `C` -> `A`!), Hex Addition (`2026_FN_Q36`: `35E80000`), Hex Multiply (`2023_FN_Q45`: `0xC15C2EF4`), and Hex Divide (`2020_FN_Q59`: `0xC0800000`).
- **Diagrams needed:** `MEMORY` (32-bit `1 | 8 | 23` field bar + 5-Region Number Line zooming near `0` & `2^{-126}`); `FRAMES` (`0x35C00000 + 0x34A00000` exponent alignment + hidden `1.` shifting, `2026_FN_Q36`; `P × Q` & `R1 / R2` exponent bias pipeline).
- **Hand traces:** Tracing `2026_AN_Q34` (`-60.25`), `2026_FN_Q36` (`35E80000`), `2025_AN_Q49` (`X=-9, Y=6, Z=12`), `2023_FN_Q45` (`0xC15C2EF4`), and `2020_FN_Q59` (`40 / -10 = -4`).
- **Common mistakes:** Forgetting the **hidden leading `1.`** before shifting mantissa right during addition (`2026_FN_Q36` gives `35C80000` or `35CC0000` traps if hidden `1` is forgotten!); forgetting `±0` / denormals sit at `E = 0` when picking smallest *normalized* (`2021_AN_Q17`!); adding biased exponents `E_1 + E_2` without subtracting `127`!
