# Open the app inside Telegram (Mini App) and branch deep links

This does not need Premium, but it is what makes your channels convert, so it is part of this plan.

## BotFather steps (phone, 5 min)
1. Open **@BotFather**, send `/mybots`, pick your bot, **Bot Settings**, **Menu Button**, **Configure menu button**.
2. URL: `https://gate.renyxera.workers.dev/?utm_source=telegram&utm_medium=bot_menu`
3. Button text: `Practise now`.
4. `/setcommands`, then send this list:

```
start - Choose your branch
cs - GATE CS channel
ece - GATE ECE channel
ee - GATE EE channel
me - GATE ME channel
civil - GATE Civil channel
da - GATE DA channel
plans - Plus and Pro
```

5. `/setdescription`: `GATE 2027 practice: official PYQs, mocks and an AI mentor. Tap Practise now.`
6. Tell me "BotFather done". I then add `/start <branch>` deep links (`t.me/<bot>?start=cs`) that reply with that branch's channel, topic and practice link, plus the `/cs`, `/ece` commands.

## Where to use the links
Instagram bio, YouTube description and story link stickers can use `t.me/<bot>?start=cs` per branch.
