# Boosts: put your 4 Premium boosts to work

**Facts:** a Premium account gives 4 boosts. You can put them all on one channel or split them, and move a boost to another channel once every 24 hours. They last while Premium is active. A channel needs a small share of its subscribers in boosts for level 1 (Telegram's FAQ says about 0.4%, "currently"): **read the live number in the app**.

## Steps (phone)
1. Open the **hub channel**, tap its name, find **Boost channel** / **Boosts** (rocket icon), tap **Boost Channel**.
2. The screen shows "Level X, N boosts to next level". Add boosts and watch the level bar.
3. Suggested split: 2 boosts on the hub, 2 on the CS channel (largest audience). Every 24 h, move boosts to whichever channel is closest to its next level.
4. Ask friends with Premium to boost the hub too. The same screen has **Copy boost link** (`t.me/boost/<channel>`). Post it once in the discussion group with a thank-you.
5. Check **Boosts → Features** to see what the current level allows and what the next adds.

## Which channels first
Hub (every student sees it), then CS, then the next largest. Concentrate boosts: a channel needs a threshold, so one boost per channel on six channels unlocks nothing.
