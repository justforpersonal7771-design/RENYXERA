# Telegram Business: a support inbox in your DMs

**Check first:** Settings, look for **Telegram Business**. I could not confirm from Telegram's pages whether it is included in your Premium plan or region, so continue only if the menu is there.

## Steps
1. **Business Hours:** 9:00–21:00 IST, Mon–Sat.
2. **Greeting Message:** `Hi! This is RENYXERA support. Tell me your GATE branch and what you need (payment, answer not loading, plan). We reply within a day.`
3. **Away Message** (outside hours): `Thanks! We are offline now and will reply tomorrow. Most answers are on gate.renyxera.workers.dev/contact`
4. **Quick Replies** (type `/` in a chat): `/pay` payment help, `/answer` "answer not loading: refresh, sign in once, send the question link", `/plans` Plus vs Pro, `/refund` refund policy link.
5. **Chatbots** (if shown): connect our bot to answer new chats. Tell me and I add the replies and the human hand-off.
6. **Labels:** `Payment`, `Bug`, `Partner`, so nothing is lost.

## Safety
Never ask students for card details or OTPs in chat. Payments happen only on the website (Razorpay).
