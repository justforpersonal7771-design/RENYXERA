# RENYXERA ad v5: "The Margin" (treatment)

Written 11 Nov 2026. Replaces AD-PLAN-V4 (which used app screenshots and no voice). Working files: `ad/reel/exam-day/` (git-ignored).

## The idea

Every GATE student has the same notebook: printed answer keys, red-pen circles, "WHY??" scrawled in the margin, a doodle when they are stuck. The whole ad is drawn on that notebook page. The red pen is the student's frustration; RENYXERA arrives as pastel cards taped onto the page, and the pen's notes change from "WHY?" to "oh. THAT is why."

- **One subject:** the student's red pen. **One goal:** to know why. **One turn:** the pen stops asking and starts ticking.
- **Native move:** everything is written, circled, highlighted, taped or stamped. Transitions are page turns, a highlighter swipe that wipes the frame, and a card being taped down.
- **No app screenshots.** The product is drawn as its own pastel cards with ink outlines, so it still reads as RENYXERA.
- **Ending that echoes:** the opening circles "(B)" in anger; the ending circles "Start free" the same way.

## Sound

- **Foley from the materials on screen:** pen scratch on paper, highlighter squeak, tape tear and press, page turn, rubber-stamp thud, pencil taps. Every visible action has its sound.
- **Score with real sampled instruments** (free sample libraries, not synth presets): upright bass, tabla and brushed percussion, felt piano, a pizzicato lift in the plans section. The clock tick of the exam timer becomes the hi-hat in shot 4.
- **Two real silences:** end of shot 2 (before the turn) and before the stamp.
- Music ducks about 10 dB under the voice. Master at −14 LUFS.

## Voice

Three test reads are in `ad/reel/exam-day/audio/vo_test/` (Indian English, female, male, expressive female). These are Microsoft neural voices: natural, but a trained ear can still tell. The only way to be certain it does not sound generated is a human read: record the script on a phone in a quiet room (a cupboard full of clothes works), and the film is timed to that recording.
