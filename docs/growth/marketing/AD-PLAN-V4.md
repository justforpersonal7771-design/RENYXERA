# RENYXERA ad v4: plan and shot list (for owner sign-off)

Written 11 Nov 2026. Replaces the v3 build (words on flat colour, almost no product on screen). Nothing is built until this is approved.

## What was wrong with v3

- Five near-static frames in 27 seconds: one line of type on one flat field at a time.
- The product is never shown. The CheQ reference shows its product or its world in almost every shot.
- No depth, no layering, no camera: nothing moves except type appearing.
- About 2 s per scene with nothing new inside the scene. The reference changes something every 1.5 s.

Not copied: their logos, photography, wording, 3D coin renders.

## Format

- 30 seconds, 60 fps. Vertical 9:16 first (Instagram, YouTube Shorts), then 16:9 and 1:1 recomposed, not cropped.
- No voice-over. Type, music and sound effects carry it.
- Music synthesised in code at 120 BPM (one beat = 0.5 s, 60 beats in the film).
- Fonts: Sora (display), Manrope (interface), the RENYXERA wordmark in Bruno Ace SC. Product colours from the design system.
- Product on screen is the real site: fresh screenshots and section crops of the live pages, moved, masked and cropped. No invented screens.

## How it gets built and checked

1. Install the missing tools (ffmpeg and uv are not on this machine; Playwright is).
2. Capture fresh screenshots and section crops of the live site, light mode, phone and desktop.
3. Synthesise the score and measure the beat grid.
4. Build the film as code where every frame is a pure function of time, with real springs and sub-frame motion blur.
5. Review loop, at least three rounds: contact sheets, phone-size sheets, strips across every transition, and short rendered clips of each section so the motion itself is judged, not only stills. Score hook, phone readability, motion, variety, brand accuracy and sound sync; fix the worst three each round until every score is 8 or more.
6. Render 9:16, 16:9 and 1:1 with mastered audio and a poster frame.
