# Instagram influencer outreach: messages you can send

How to use this file: pick the message that matches the creator, replace the words in [BRACKETS], and send it from your own account. Send 15 to 20 a day at most, never the same text twice in a row, and never send a link in the very first message to a stranger (Instagram often hides those). The first message only needs a reply. Share the link after they answer.

Your facts to fill in:
- [LINK] your site link (use your tracking link, see `docs/GROWTH_TASKS.md`)
- [CODE] a coupon code you create for that creator (see the top of migration 0036 for how to make one), so you can see which creator brings buyers
- [YOUR NAME] Adil

## 1. Who to message

| Type | Followers | What to offer | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Micro GATE creators | 2k to 30k | Free Plus for 3 months and a coupon for their followers | They answer, and their followers are exactly your students |
| Subject teachers and YouTubers | 10k to 100k | Free Pro, a listing in your tools, a shout-out | Trust. One mention is worth ten ads |
| Study-motivation pages | 20k to 200k | A flat fee or free Pro plus a code | Reach, but less targeted |
| Toppers and last-year rankers | any | Free Pro for honest feedback | Real proof for your page |

Start with the first two rows. They cost you nothing but free plans.

## 2. First message (no link). Pick one

**A. Short and friendly**
> Hi [NAME], I watch your GATE [SUBJECT] videos. The [SPECIFIC VIDEO] one helped me. I built a free GATE practice site with real past papers, a study planner and Telegram reminders. Can I send you a free Pro account to try? No strings. 🙂

**B. For a teacher**
> Hi sir/ma'am [NAME], your explanation of [TOPIC] is the clearest I have seen. I am building RENYXERA, a GATE practice app with official past-year questions, topic-wise analytics and a planner. Would you be open to trying it and telling me what is missing? I would give you Pro for free.

**C. For a motivation page**
> Hi [NAME]! Love your GATE content. I run RENYXERA, a free GATE prep site (past papers, mocks, planner). I would like to give your followers a special code. Do you take collaborations? I can share details.

**D. For a topper**
> Hi [NAME], congratulations on your GATE rank! I built a practice site and I would love your honest view on what a student really needs in the last 60 days. Can I give you a free Pro account and ask 3 quick questions?

## 3. Second message (after they reply)

> Great, thank you! Here is the site: [LINK]. Sign up and I will upgrade your account to Pro today. What are three things you would want a GATE student to do on it first? I can also make a code like [CODE] so your followers get a discount. 

If they ask what is in it:
> Real GATE past papers with solutions, practice by section, subject or topic, All-India mocks with rank, a study planner that builds a schedule around your free hours, Telegram reminders, analytics on your weak topics, and a free score and cut-off calculator. Free to start, Plus is ₹29 a month.

## 4. Collaboration offer (when they are interested)

> Here is what I can offer:
> 1. Free Pro for you for [3] months.
> 2. A personal code [CODE] giving your followers [20]% off Plus and Pro.
> 3. A shout-out on our Telegram channel and a "recommended by" mention on our site.
> 4. If you make a reel or story, I will send you [AMOUNT] after it goes live. [Only if you can afford it. Free plans are enough for micro creators.]
> What would you like to do?

## 5. What to ask the creator to post (give them this)

Three easy ideas, each 20 to 30 seconds:
1. "Free GATE tools I would use if I were starting today": show the score calculator, the cut-off page and the General Aptitude lessons.
2. "My GATE planner in 30 seconds": show the planner building a plan around free hours.
3. "How many marks do I need?": show the branch cut-offs page and the calculator with their own marks.

Caption starter they can copy:
> Free GATE prep tools I found this week 👇 past papers, planner, score calculator. Link in bio. Use code [CODE]. #GATE2027 #GATEprep #GATECSE

## 6. Follow-up messages (send once each, three days apart)

**Day 3**
> Hi [NAME], just checking if you had a chance to open the site. Happy to answer any question or fix anything you find. 🙂

**Day 7**
> No pressure at all. If it is not a fit, I understand. If you ever want a free account for your students or a custom code, it is yours. Thanks for your time!

Then stop. Do not message a third time.

## 7. Replies to common answers

**"What do you charge?" or "Is this paid promotion?"**
> I can give you free Pro and a code for your followers. If you work with fees, tell me your rate for one reel and I will tell you honestly if it fits my budget.

**"Send details."**
> Sure. RENYXERA is a GATE prep platform: official past papers, mocks with All-India rank, a study planner, Telegram reminders and analytics. I will send a short demo video and a Pro account. What email should I use?

**"My followers are not interested in paid things."**
> Understood. The core is free: past papers, calculator, planner. I would only share the free tools.

## 9. Rules to protect your account and your name

- Disclose paid or free-account collaborations (Instagram "paid partnership" label). It is the law in India for influencers and it builds trust.
- Never promise a rank or a score. Say "practice" and "track", not "guarantee".
- Never buy followers or comments. It kills reach and trust.
- Track every message in your sheet (G-5 in `docs/GROWTH_TASKS.md`): name, handle, date, status, code.
