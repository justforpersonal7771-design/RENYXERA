# Feature ideas for the whole app (living backlog)

*Started 31 Oct 2026 at the owner's request: ideas for any part of RENYXERA, not only battles. Add new ideas at the bottom of each section as they come. Each line: **idea**, who it helps, rough effort (S = days, M = about a week, L = weeks), and any rule it must respect. Nothing here is promised until it moves to `checklist.md`. Rules for all: zero budget, answers graded on the server, "tease, don't block" for paid features, phone first.*

## Play and compete
- **Arena** live rooms (see `roadmap/R25-BATTLE-ARENA.md` and `R25-ARENA-UX-DESIGN.md`). L
- **Daily Duel**: one question a day, a global speed-and-accuracy board, all branches. S to M
- **Friend leagues**: weekly ladder among a small group, promotion and relegation like Duolingo. M
- **Boss of the week**: one monstrous multi-concept question the whole community attacks; hint ladder unlocks as total attempts grow. M
- **Revenge match**: one tap to re-challenge whoever beat you, on the topics where you lost. S
- **Study-buddy mode**: two students share a timer and a scoreboard against the clock (co-op, no loser). M

## Learn and understand
- **Explain it back**: write or say a 2-line explanation of a topic; the Mentor marks gaps. M
- **Concept snap**: a deck of 20 one-line facts per chapter swiped as true/false with a trap twist. S
- **Teach mode**: the student sets a question for a friend and sees how many got it (the Arena Setter idea, outside rooms). M
- **Trap library**: every typical wrong answer from the PYQs with why it tempts, searchable. M
- **Formula drill**: 60-second rapid recall of the formulas for a subject, spaced by how you did. S
- **Simulators** (cache, paging, scheduling, DFA, K-map, B+ tree) inside lessons (see Study future features). M each

## Plan and stay on track
- **Why you are stuck**: after a bad week, the Mentor looks at mistakes and suggests two changes, not ten. M
- **Exam-day mode**: the last 7 days shrink the app to formula sheets, traps and light revision, no new topics. S
- **Mock readiness meter**: combines accuracy, speed and coverage into one honest number with the three levers behind it. M
- **Time-boxed sessions with a finish line**: "25 minutes, 12 questions, then stop" with a visible end so it never feels endless. S
- **Weekly letter**: a Sunday summary in Telegram and the app: wins, one weak topic, next week's plan. S

## Motivation and habit
- **Streak freeze earned by revision** (not bought). S
- **Seasons**: a monthly theme, a badge set and a leaderboard reset, aligned with the Telegram channel posts. M
- **Study rooms**: silent "library" rooms where friends see each other studying (a presence dot, a shared timer), no chat. M
- **Milestone cards**: a shareable card when a topic reaches 80 % mastery (consent first, per the sharing rules). S

## Phone-first and offline
- **Install prompt and iPhone card** (R24). S
- **Photo doubts**: photograph a question and ask the Mentor. M to L (needs the quota plan)
- **Widgets and notification actions** (later, needs a native wrapper). L
- **Commute mode**: audio recall cards and a one-handed swipe quiz. M

## Community and trust
- **Report a mistake** on any question or lesson paragraph with a visible "fixed" note. S
- **Reviewer badges**: a few trusted toppers verify solutions and are credited. M
- **Question of the week from the community**: the best Setter-written question (from Arena) becomes a Forge candidate after review. M

## Business and growth
- **Bundle suggestions at the right moments** (see `study-materials/13-PRICING-BUNDLES-AND-OFFERS.md`). M
- **Referral squads**: a group that studies together unlocks a group reward when all hit a goal. M
- **College and coaching mode**: a teacher creates a private room and sees a class heatmap (a possible paid tier later). L
