PART 1. WHAT RENYXERA IS
======================================================================
- A web app (installable PWA, phone and desktop) for Indian engineering students preparing for GATE (Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering), a national computer-based postgraduate entrance exam. Tagline: "A new ERA of intelligent learning". Old internal name: GATE OS.
- Live at https://gate.renyxera.workers.dev . No custom domain yet (deferred until revenue).
- Owner: a non-developer product owner. All engineering is done by an AI coding assistant (Claude Code) in local sessions. The owner runs database migrations and account setups himself.
- Stage: live, early. About 1,200 visitors in the 30 days to 6 Nov 2026; about 7% start a test; 0 paying customers so far (only the owner's own test payments).
- GATE facts: 65 questions, 100 marks, 3 hours. Question types: MCQ (single answer; wrong 1-mark = -1/3, wrong 2-mark = -2/3), MSQ (multiple correct, no negative, all-or-nothing), NAT (numeric answer within a range, no negative). Papers live in the app: CS, EC, EE, ME, CE, DA. GATE 2027 runs 6 to 21 Feb 2027 (IIT Madras). Results usually mid-March; March is also when the app refreshes official data.
- Audience: 20 to 26 years old, final-year students and graduates (some working), mostly studying alone, often at night, mostly on phones, cost-sensitive (coaching is expensive), English-medium for now (Hindi later).

PART 10. HOW TO MAKE FEATURES THAT DO NOT LOOK AI-DEVELOPED (give advice in this frame)
======================================================================
1. Start from the subject's world, not a template: exam hall, answer sheets, OMR bubbles, red pen, calculator keys, circuit boards, lab notebooks, rank lists, cut-off tables. Pull shapes, textures and copy from there.
2. One signature element per screen (for example the readiness gauge, the question palette, the VS intro), everything else quiet.
3. Hierarchy by size + weight + colour, not by boxing everything in cards. Vary density and rhythm; avoid identical card grids.
4. Real data and real copy in plain words (sentence case, verbs that say what happens, no "Unlock your potential").
5. Motion that answers an action (press, open, confirm, reward), with physical easing; one orchestrated moment per screen; reduced-motion versions.
6. Colour used for meaning (status, identity, reward), not decoration; one accent per surface; dark mode built as its own system (elevation by lightness, desaturated accents).
7. Finish details: tabular numbers, optical alignment, empty/loading/error states designed, focus rings, hit areas 44 px, concentric radii.
8. Taste check: would another AI produce the same screen from the same prompt? If yes, change the idea, not the colour.
